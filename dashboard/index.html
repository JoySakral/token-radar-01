How the model works

  1. Base. TVL × 0.25 — a protocol's locked value is the anchor of what its token could be worth.
  2. Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, moves the base by half its size.
  3. Maturity. Time listed with no token recorded, from 0.60 for a brand-new listing to 1.00 at three years.
  4. Breadth. More chains means wider distribution: 1 + 0.05 × ln(chains), capped at 1.15.
  5. Momentum. Growing faster than 25% in 7 days while under two years old earns a 1.05 boost.

Implied value is this radar's own estimate, not a price and not a prediction that a token will be issued. “Records no token” means the snapshot's record carries no token — a protocol may still have one its record does not show. Farm steps are how to use a protocol and position for a possible future token; they never claim a confirmed airdrop, a date, or a price.
